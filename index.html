About Aura Humanitas

Academic research with a humanistic perspective.

Aura Humanitas is an academic and popular humanities journal designed to publish original research, scholarly essays, reviews, and interdisciplinary studies concerned with human culture, history, philosophy, literature, art, society, and related fields.

For Researchers

Author Guidelines

01

Manuscript Preparation

Prepare an original manuscript with a clear title, author information, abstract, keywords, main text, references, and any necessary tables or figures. Submit the manuscript in Microsoft Word format so the editorial office can process it efficiently.

02

Submission

Submit the required author and manuscript information through the online submission form and upload the manuscript in Microsoft Word format. Authors should provide accurate contact and affiliation details and ensure that the submitted file is the correct version.

03

Peer Review

Submissions are assessed by the editorial office for scope, completeness, originality, and academic suitability before peer review. Suitable manuscripts are evaluated by qualified reviewers, and editorial decisions are based on the scholarly quality and relevance of the work.

04

Publication Ethics

Authors are responsible for originality, proper attribution, accurate authorship, disclosure of conflicts of interest, and research integrity. The journal follows recognized publication-ethics principles and may request corrections, clarifications, or revisions when necessary.
