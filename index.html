About the Journal

Journal of Aura Humanitas is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal managed by an independent editorial team. It welcomes high-quality original research, review articles and short communications across the humanities, social sciences, education and related fields.

The journal aims to support responsible scholarship, interdisciplinary dialogue and the exchange of ideas across regions and research communities.
